High-Yield Study & Revision Tips

Scientifically backed learning methods to maximize retention for college semester exams.

💡 Active Recall Method

Technique

Instead of passively rereading lecture notes or highlighting textbook lines, close your notebook and try to write down the core concepts, derivations, or code syntax from pure memory.

Best for: Formulas, definitions, and code syntax.

📅 Spaced Repetition

Planning

Review difficult topics at increasing intervals (Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14). This prevents the natural forgetting curve and embeds difficult engineering theorems into long-term memory.

Best for: Semester exam preparation & finals.

⏱ The 50/10 Focus Cycle

Productivity

Work with full concentration for 50 uninterrupted minutes with your phone out of sight. Follow this strictly with a 10-minute break away from screens to let your mind consolidate what you studied.

Best for: Long laboratory and problem-solving sessions.

📝 Formula Sheet Compression

Revision

Condense an entire semester course onto a single double-sided handwritten cheat sheet. The process of deciding what is important enough to include forces deep conceptual synthesis.

Best for: Midterms and quick revision before entering the exam hall.
